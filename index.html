Current research Undergraduate thesis
Ongoing

Sensitive Data Protection via Context-Aware Prompt Sanitization in Web-Based LLM Chatbots

My undergraduate thesis explores how a privacy gateway can protect sensitive information in prompts before they reach an LLM. I’m investigating how to detect and rewrite sensitive content while preserving the usefulness of the original prompt.

Research interests

Directions I’d like to explore further.

/ 01

Tool use and reliable AI agents

I’m interested in how language-model agents use tools to carry out practical tasks from choosing and calling tools to coordinating workflows and handling failures.

/ 02

Agent evaluation and reliability

I’m interested in how we evaluate whether agents complete tasks reliably, check their results, and recognize when they need human input.

/ 03

Privacy in AI systems

I’m interested in protecting sensitive information in AI systems while preserving their usefulness. My current thesis explores this through context-aware prompt sanitization in LLM chatbots.
